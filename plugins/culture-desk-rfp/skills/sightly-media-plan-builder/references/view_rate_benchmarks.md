# View Rate Benchmarks by Platform and Placement

This is a fallback reference for the skill's view-rate inputs when the Sightly Tactics Playbooks (`13_Tactics_Playbook_YouTube.md` and siblings) are not available in the working directory. If the Tactics Playbook is available, prefer that — it's the source of truth and the values may be more current.

These benchmarks are standard expected view rates for upper-funnel, awareness-objective video placements in US national campaigns. They are not guarantees, and they do not apply to lower-funnel or conversion-optimized buys where view-rate behavior is different.

## Usage notes

1. **These are upper-funnel awareness benchmarks.** Lower-funnel and conversion-optimized buys often see lower view rates because the algorithm is trading view-completion for action signal. For Tier 2 conversion campaigns, expect view rates 5–10 percentage points below these numbers.
2. **If the strategist provides a view rate, use the strategist's number.** This reference is only a fallback when the strategist hasn't supplied one and the Tactics Playbook isn't accessible.
3. **If a placement isn't listed here**, do not invent a number. Ask the strategist explicitly, or leave the ESTIMATED VIEW RATE blank with a NOTES annotation explaining why.
4. **View rate is decimal in the CSV cell**: `0.27`, not `27%`. The strategist's spreadsheet handles the percent formatting.
