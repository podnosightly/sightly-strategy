# The Fixed Five-Slide Layout

This is the static physical build for the Sightly self-service proposal deck. It does not change per campaign. The narrative arc, the story and emphasis, is built fresh from each brief and mapped into these five slides in this order. Do not add, drop, or reorder slides. Vary the words, never the structure.

Each slide below lists its fixed structural elements: the constant lines that are part of the template's DNA (reproduce these, filling the brackets), the section headers and field labels (keep verbatim), and the copy logic for the parts that flex per campaign.

---

## Slide 1 — Intelligence Intro

**Fixed opener (reproduce, fill brackets):**
"Before a dollar is spent, Brand Mentality® is already reading the [category] conversation across social, news, and video. Here is what it is surfacing for [client], what it tells us, and the strategy it points to."

**Fixed header:** "What this unlocks for [client]:"

**Flexes per campaign:** three to four unlock points, each a full sentence, each pairing a capability with what it does for this client. The recurring four are: show up inside the conversation rather than beside it, move past a demographic box to the client's actual personas, catch intent when the client's actual reactive triggers spike, and connect moments to measurable outcomes. Rewrite each around this client's category, personas, and triggers.

---

## Slide 2 — Strategic Approach

**Fixed structure:** three named strategic moves, each a short header line on its own line followed by one or two sentences, then the intent-peak line, then the Sightly supply line.

**The three moves (headers flex to the category, logic is constant):**
- Be native to the [category] conversation
- Reach [personas] in the [intent] moment
- Make the [action] the obvious move

**Fixed intent-peak line (fill brackets from the moment map):**
"Intent peaks around [what]. Across this flight, that means [the named moments inside the window]."

**Fixed supply line (reproduce, adjust the platform clause to the buy):**
"Sightly supplies the Brand Profile, Anticipation Boards, and SmartLists™. Only a one-click push to your [DSP] seat."
For a badged-platform buy, replace the platform clause with the correct badged-partner phrasing (for example, the badged TikTok Marketing Partner, Media Buying Technology category line, or the badged YouTube Measurement Program (YTMP) partner line).

---

## Slide 4 — Personas

**Fixed intro line (reproduce):**
"Brand Mentality® validated the audiences from the brief and made them actionable. Inside each one is the tension they sit with, the moment that flips them, and where to reach them."

**Fixed per-persona structure:** the persona name on its own line, then prose built on three beats in this order: the tension the persona sits with, the moment that flips them, and where to reach them. One block per confirmed persona. Keep persona names and roles identical to the locked strategy.

---

## Slide 5 — Content Universe

**Fixed opener shape (fill brackets):**
"[Personas] are not only [the obvious official content]."

**Flexes:** the full content universe Brand Mentality® activates across for this client, named specifically: the content types, formats, communities, and conversation moments the strategy identified, resolving to when both audiences decide it is finally time to tune in. This is where the breadth of the SmartLists™ build shows.

---

## Overriding the layout

This layout is the default and the norm. If the strategist explicitly pastes a different template or asks for a different structure for a specific deck, follow their structure for that deck. Absent that, this five-slide layout is the build every time.
