# Sightly Budget Minimums — Feasibility Reference

This is the feasibility gate the media plan builder consults in Step A. Embedded here so the skill works without needing the broader Sightly knowledge base. If `05_Budget_Minimums_and_Feasibility.md` is available in the knowledge base, prefer it: it's the source of truth and may be more recent. This copy follows `05` as of 12 November 2026.

## One list

These are the entry-level monthly minimums. The old three-tier breakout was retired on 12 November 2026 (Matt McConnell), and the figures below are the old Tier 1 figures. Sightly's minimums are recommendations, confirmed case by case with the Account Management and Performance Management teams; a client can knowingly run below one once the flag is raised.

## Which column to use

**Compare against the Gross column.** The client's budget is a gross (billing) figure and the plan sums to it, so the gross minimums are the live gate, compared per platform, per month. The Net column is kept for reference only; never convert a client budget to net to run this check.

Gross figures assume a 30% margin. Where the deal's margin differs (some plans run nearer 15%), recompute the gross minimum as net ÷ (1 − margin) and compare against that (`05`; `14_Open_Items.md`). This applies to the minimum only; never convert the client's budget.

## Platform-specific minimums (additional)

- **TikTok** (platform-enforced, a hard stop): each ad group's daily budget must exceed $20, and a lifetime budget must be at least days × $20 (a 31-day ad group needs $620+); a campaign budget must exceed $50. Each ad group needs more than $20 a day, so count the ad groups before splitting the budget. TikTok's public guidance of $200 a day for delivery stability is a recommendation; treat it like a Sightly minimum.
- **Spotify**: Minimum $250 per campaign. Managed-service threshold is $25K.
- **Every line**: $100 a day per line item ($50 a day for underfunded local markets). A Sightly recommendation; flag lines below it.
- **Tactic count**: under $10K, one tactic; $10K–25K, two; $25K+, three. If we split below these thresholds, each line falls further under $100 a day. The source doesn't record whether the thresholds are monthly or per flight, so we judge by the daily figure (`05` › Line-item floors and tactic count).

## Usage rules

1. **Always check minimums before building a plan.** This is the first feasibility gate.
2. **Always call out when a plan falls below minimums.** Include the specific platform and the minimum it misses, and say which kind it is: platform-enforced or Sightly's recommendation.
3. **Explain the consequence**, not just the number — insufficient delivery, inability to optimize, lack of statistical significance, learning-period risk.
4. **Client overrides are acceptable** when the callout has been made and acknowledged. The flag is what matters.
5. **Never silently plan below the floor.** An underfunded line that has been flagged and discussed is fine. One that was never mentioned is a planning failure.
